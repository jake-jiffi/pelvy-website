Locked-off overhead tripod shot, the camera does not move at all, no zoom, no pan, no drift. A pale timber clinic desk in the morning. Sunlight comes through a window with a tree outside: a broad band of warm light and the soft moving shadows of leaves slide slowly across the whole desk from left to right, pass over the white card and the ceramic dish, then slide back to where they began. The blank white card in the centre, the ceramic dish, the notebooks and the pen all stay perfectly still. The card stays plain blank white with nothing printed on it. Slow, calm, natural light. No people, no hands, no text, no logos.
