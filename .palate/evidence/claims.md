## Deliberately left off

- "If your clinic joins later, your clinician's programs and notes appear in the same app": written in the brand voice guide, but nothing in Pelvy's own material confirms the app does this. Removed from the site on 12 October 2026 after the final critique flagged it.
- "Patients remember less than 20%" and "forget up to 80%": unsourced on the live site (brand decision d3).
- "Five times the adherence": said in the demo video, no data shown (brand q10).
- "Save 30+ minutes every day", "onboard in less than 20 minutes", "no lock-in contracts": overlays in the "When Clinicians Subscribe" video, no source.
- The home page testimonials (Courtney Henry, Cody Fisher, Devon Lane) and sixteen client logos: template placeholders (brand q6).
- App Store and Play ratings: true on 12 October 2026, but they date.
- $99 a year and "not yet, join the waitlist" from the old patient FAQ: superseded by the app (brand e38, d24).
- "Track. Treat. Thrive.": the old line, and "treat" is a claim Pelvy cannot make (brand q8).
