We are excited to share that we had our first launch party in March!

I went to Perth and I was so excited to throw a green themed gathering to celebrate with physios in WA and to finally meet some of our subscribing clinicians in person. Not everyone could attend – but all of these practice owners have onboarded themselves and their staff since we launched Pelvy’s Pilot in November last year. I was thrilled to meet [Taryn Watson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/taryn-watson-471274aa/) and [Agner Grieco Hazewinkel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/agner-grieco-hazewinkel-11a58a1b0/) from [FitRight Women’s Health](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fitrightwomenshealth/), Michelle Perks from [PhysioSista Pelvic Health Centre](https://www.linkedin.com/company/physiosista-pelvic-health-centre/), and Kate Fosdike from Lift Pelvic Health.

They use Pelvy every single day with their patients and I loved giving them their welcome packs in person with their “We support our patients with Pelvy” stickers for their clinics and their beautiful poo charts with the best defecation picture and a QR code to make it even quicker for patients to download Pelvy in consults.

This is just our first launch party and my goal is to be able to do one in each state this year. Connecting and meeting clinicians that are so passionate about supporting their patients has actually been such a highlight of making Pelvy. We have so much in common and I feel like I know so many of these women to extent that I wish we lived closer to be friends!

Their feedback continues to shape the clinician platform and the Pelvy app. I can’t wait to feature every clinic that has been part of this journey as our social media campaign takes off. We will also be making a Clinician page on this website so people can easily search for a pelvic health physiotherapist that subscribes to Pelvy in their area.

Thank you [Danielle Lewis](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lewisdaniellec/) – marketing wiz and founder extraordinaire from [SPARK Business](https://www.linkedin.com/company/spark-business-global/) who also came – I couldn’t be in Perth without meeting her in person and it was a dream come true for her to come to our WA launch party

To the other clinics in Perth or WA – I’m sorry I missed you but thanks to having family in Perth, I’ll definitely be back again.
